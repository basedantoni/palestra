# TCG catalog from punk-records, artists from Limitless, prices by hand

> Superseded on prices and exchange rates by [0007](./0007-market-watch-price-sources.md). The catalog, artist and image parts stand.

As of October 2026 no sanctioned price feed exists for Japanese One Piece singles: TCGplayer has stopped granting API access and carries no Japanese One Piece, Cardmarket is not accepting API applications, and Japanese shops (Yuyu-tei, Card Rush) and Mercari have no API, with Yuyu-tei's terms forbidding reproduction. So Offers are entered by hand in USD for both languages; do not add a price sync without re-checking these. TCGCSV's free mirror of TCGplayer prices covers English only and gives a market average rather than a seller's delivered price, so it was declined as a reference. Bandai's card lists do not credit illustrators, so the Japanese and English catalogs are imported from punk-records (a static mirror of Bandai's lists, keyed by Bandai's variant ID such as `OP05-069_p1`) and artists are backfilled once per set from Limitless One Piece, the only source crediting artists per variant; an Artwork Limitless leaves uncredited stays blank. One variant ID is treated as one Artwork across both languages. That is assumed, not verified for every card, so the import flags any variant ID whose languages disagree on artist instead of merging silently. Card images stay on Bandai's servers and are never copied.

## Amended (October 2026, after KOI-304 shipped)

- **Images are relayed, not hotlinked.** Bandai sends `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: same-site`, so browsers refuse its images on our pages. The server relays them through `GET /api/tcg/image`. The route is signed-in only and fetches only Bandai card-image URLs. It refuses redirects, times out after 10 s and stores nothing; responses carry a 7-day private cache header.
- **The one-variant-ID-one-Artwork assumption fails for some reprints.** Bandai's Japanese and English lists can number the same print differently: OP05-119's PRB-01 print is `_p3` in Japanese and `_p4` in English. Limitless numbers its Japanese and English pages the same way, so both languages report the same artist and the conflict flag stays off. Those Japanese Printings can show another Artwork's artist. Japanese reprint IDs (`_rN`) never appear on Limitless and stay uncredited.

## Amended (October 2026, KOI-355): English pairs to Japanese by pack and image

The October amendment above understated the problem: Bandai's English suffixes drift from the Japanese ones after reprints and promos, and at least 338 English variants were merged with the wrong Japanese Artwork. Limitless doesn't help either: it has its own numbering, shared by both languages and matching neither Bandai list (its OP05-119 `_p3` is Bandai's Japanese `_p5`). So the import no longer treats one variant ID as one Artwork across languages. An Artwork is identified by its Japanese variant ID, and each Printing keeps its own language's list ID (`list_id`). English Printings are paired to Japanese Artworks in tiers ([research](https://github.com/basedantoni/palestra/blob/research/en-ja-pairing/docs/research/en-ja-pairing.md): 99.7% on the 136 shift-suspect cards, against 59.5% for matching IDs):

1. **Pack groups.** One English and one Japanese variant of a card in the same pack pair directly. Promotion card (`x901`) and Other Product Card (`x801`) packs are excluded.
2. **Art distance** on Bandai's card images, within the pack first and then across packs, with list order breaking identical-art ties. The distance is 1 − the median Pearson r over 16 z-normalised blocks of the illustration band in 64×64 grayscale. Below 0.20 is the same art, 0.20–0.60 is a gray zone, and above 0.60 is different art. Images are fetched transiently and decoded with `pngjs`. Neither the images nor their signatures are kept, only the resulting pairing (the Printing's Artwork), so a re-import fetches images only for new or unpaired English records.
3. **Manual overrides** in a checked-in list, for gray-zone pairs (stamped promos) and twin contests (several identical-image promos on one side). Until a pair is reviewed, the import takes its best guess and reports it.
4. **Unpaired after all tiers** means a one-language print. An English-only print becomes its own Artwork under its English list ID with an `EN-` prefix, so it can't collide with a later Japanese ID. Korea-only and China-only prints are still not Artworks: no catalog lists them.

Considered and rejected: list order within a pack alone (Bandai orders some packs differently per language, and EB-04 sits in different packs), image alone (reprints in other packs share one scan), Limitless numbering, and Marketplace data (TCGCSV has no suffixes, and CardTrader's letters don't follow Bandai's). The import refuses to delete an Artwork that carries user data. Re-pairing updates English Printings in place, so Copies and Offers follow the physical print. Artist backfill from Limitless is still keyed by Limitless's numbering, so it mis-credits shifted cards; that is tracked as its own bug.
